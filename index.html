How to play

Each day gives you five chances to place a hidden film credit on a narrowing timeline.

  1. Study the fully revealed anchor and the clue on the other side.
  2. Drag or tap the line to place a marker in value modes, or choose a card in Middle mode.
  3. Place your marker, lock it in, and watch the line contract for the next round.

Middle mode changes the question: choose the film credit you think sits closest to the midpoint. The release years stay hidden until you lock in.

Your Deviate score is your average miss across the five rounds, scaled to each round's line. Lower is better: 0.0 is perfect. Placement accuracy shows the same result the other way around: 100% is perfect.

Progress and stats stay in this browser. Use the export button in Stats if you want a backup.
