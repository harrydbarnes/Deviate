How to play

Each day gives you five chances to place a hidden film credit on a narrowing timeline.

  1. Study the fully revealed anchor and the clue on the other side.
  2. Drag or tap the marker where you think the hidden value belongs.
  3. Lock in your guess. The answer is revealed and the range contracts for the next round.

Your score is the average distance from the truth, normalised to each round's range. Lower is better. A perfect game is 0.0.

Progress and stats stay in this browser. Use the export button in Stats if you want a backup.
